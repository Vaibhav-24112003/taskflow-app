Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2026

1. Subscriptions

TaskFlowCo is offered on a subscription basis (monthly or yearly). Fees are charged in advance for each billing cycle through our payment gateway (Razorpay) and are exclusive of applicable taxes (e.g. GST), which are added.

2. Free trial

Where a free trial or introductory offer is provided, you are not charged for the trial period. Paid features lock when the trial ends unless you subscribe. Any nominal verification charge (if applicable) is disclosed at checkout.

3. Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time from Plans & Billing in the app, or by emailing support@taskflowco.in.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan remains active until the end of the current paid cycle, after which paid features lock and the account moves to read-only/free access.
  • We do not automatically pro-rate or refund the unused portion of a cycle except as stated below or required by law.

4. Refunds

  • First-time purchases: if you are dissatisfied, you may request a refund within 7 days of your first payment, provided the Service has not been substantially used/abused. Approved refunds are for the current cycle only.
  • Renewals: renewal payments are generally non-refundable. If a renewal was charged in error or you cancelled before renewal but were still charged, contact us and we will investigate and refund if appropriate.
  • Duplicate/failed transactions: any duplicate charge or amount debited without service activation will be refunded in full.
  • Refunds are made to the original payment method via Razorpay and typically settle within 5–10 business days after approval.
  • Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@taskflowco.in from your registered email with your organisation name and payment/invoice reference. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this page. Material changes will be notified in-app or by email.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 April 2026 · Applies to taskflowco.in and the TaskFlowCo apps

TaskFlowCo is a practice-management platform for CA, CS, CMA and tax firms ("Firms"). Firms use TaskFlowCo to manage their own clients' compliance work. This means we handle two kinds of personal data with two different roles under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"):
  • Firm & user account data — where we act as a Data Fiduciary (we decide how it is processed).
  • Your clients' data that a Firm uploads (e.g. PAN, GSTIN, financial figures, documents, portal credentials) — where we act as a Data Processor on the Firm's instructions. The Firm is the Data Fiduciary for that data and is responsible for having a lawful basis and notice/consent to upload it. Our handling of such data is governed by our Data Processing Addendum.

1. Who we are

This platform is operated by Raj (Yash) Bhoite, Proprietor — TaskFlowCo ("TaskFlowCo", "we", "us"), a sole proprietorship, registered at A/P Shegaon, Tal. Jath, Dist. Sangli, Maharashtra – 416404. Contact: support@taskflowco.in, phone +91 97677 40136.

2. Information we collect

a) You give us

  • Account data: name, work email, phone, firm name, team size, role, password (stored only as a salted hash), profile photo.
  • Firm operational data: organisations, workspaces, tasks, worksheets, work types, attendance, time logs, announcements, and messages you create.
  • Your clients' data (as a Firm): client name, PAN, GSTIN, TAN, contact details, compliance status, due dates, uploaded documents, and — where you choose to store them — third-party portal credentials. Credential secrets are stored encrypted (see Security).
  • Demo/contact requests: details you submit through "Book a Demo" or by emailing us.
  • Payment data: processed by our payment gateway (Razorpay). We store transaction metadata (amount, plan, status, invoice) but not your full card/bank details.

b) Collected automatically

  • Device/technical data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, timestamps, and — for attendance punches you choose to record — approximate geolocation.
  • Cookies/local storage used to keep you signed in and remember preferences (see Cookies).

3. How we use your data

  • To provide, secure, and operate the platform and its features.
  • To authenticate you and enforce access controls between organisations.
  • To send transactional communications (invoices, reminders, security and service notices).
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your Firm's client data to train AI models or for advertising.

4. Legal basis (DPDP Act)

We process account and firm data on the basis of your consent and/or to perform our contract with you (to deliver the service you signed up for), and for the "legitimate uses" permitted under the DPDP Act. For your clients' data, the Firm is responsible for the lawful basis and for giving the required notice to its clients; we process it only to provide the service.

6. Cross-border transfers

Your core data is stored in India (Mumbai region). Some sub-processors above operate outside India; where data is transferred abroad we rely on the transfer mechanisms permitted under applicable law and require appropriate safeguards.

7. Security

  • Isolation: every organisation's data is separated at the database level using row-level security policies, so one Firm cannot access another Firm's data.
  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest; stored credential secrets are additionally encrypted with a key held in a secrets vault.
  • Access control: role-based access within organisations; administrative access is restricted and logged.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but work to protect your data using industry-standard measures.

8. Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. After account closure we retain data for a limited wind-down period (typically up to 90 days) to allow export/recovery, then delete or anonymise it, except where longer retention is required by law (e.g. tax/invoice records) or to resolve disputes.

9. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act, you may request to access, correct, complete, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity. To exercise these rights, contact our Grievance Officer below. For your clients' data held on behalf of a Firm, please contact the Firm (the Data Fiduciary); we will assist the Firm as its processor.

10. Cookies & local storage

We use strictly necessary cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings (such as theme and last-opened view). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear these via your browser, though some features may stop working.

11. Children

The platform is intended for professional/business use and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

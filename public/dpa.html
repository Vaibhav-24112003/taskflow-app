Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Last updated: [[EFFECTIVE DATE]] · Forms part of the Terms of Service

This DPA applies when you (the "Firm") use TaskFlowCo to process personal data of your own clients and staff. Under the DPDP Act, 2023, the Firm is the Data Fiduciary and TaskFlowCo ([[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]]) is the Data Processor acting on the Firm's documented instructions.

1. Roles & scope

TaskFlowCo processes personal data only to provide the Service and only on the Firm's instructions (which include the configuration and use of the Service, and this DPA). The Firm is responsible for the lawfulness of the data it uploads and for providing any notices/consents to its own data principals.

2. Details of processing

Subject-matterProvision of the TaskFlowCo practice-management Service.
DurationFor the term of the subscription, plus the wind-down/retention period.
Nature & purposeHosting, storage, organisation, retrieval, transmission and display of Customer Data to operate the Service.
Categories of dataIdentity & contact details; PAN/GSTIN/TAN; compliance status & deadlines; financial figures and documents uploaded by the Firm; portal login credentials (stored encrypted); staff attendance & time logs; messages.
Data principalsThe Firm's clients and their representatives; the Firm's own staff/team members.

3. Processor obligations

  • Process personal data only on the Firm's documented instructions, unless required by law (in which case we will inform the Firm unless legally prohibited).
  • Ensure personnel authorised to process data are bound by confidentiality.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (Annex B).
  • Assist the Firm, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to data-principal requests and in meeting the Firm's security, breach-notification and (where applicable) impact-assessment obligations.
  • Not sell personal data and not use Customer Data for our own purposes, advertising, or AI-model training.

5. Security

See Annex B. In summary: database-level tenant isolation (row-level security), encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted storage of credential secrets using a vault-held key, role-based access controls, and restricted, monitored administrative access.

6. Personal data breach

We will notify the Firm without undue delay (and in any event within [[72 hours]]) after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Firm's data, with the information reasonably available to help the Firm meet its own notification duties (including to the Data Protection Board of India where applicable).

7. Data-principal requests

If we receive a request from a data principal relating to the Firm's data, we will (unless legally required to respond) refer them to the Firm and assist the Firm in responding.

8. Return & deletion

On termination, the Firm may export Customer Data for a limited period. Thereafter we will delete or anonymise it within [[e.g. 60–90 days]], except where retention is required by law.

9. Audits

On reasonable prior written request (no more than once per year, subject to confidentiality), we will make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

10. International transfers

Core data is stored in India. Where a sub-processor processes data outside India, we ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

11. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Terms of Service.

Annex A — Instructions

The Firm's instructions are: to process Customer Data as necessary to provide the Service as configured by the Firm, to provide support, and as otherwise set out in the Terms and this DPA.

Annex B — Security measures

  • Per-organisation isolation enforced by database row-level security policies.
  • TLS encryption in transit; encryption at rest for the database and file storage.
  • Credential secrets encrypted with a key stored in a secrets vault, decrypted only by authorised server-side functions that verify the caller's organisation membership.
  • Role-based access control within organisations; principle of least privilege for internal access.
  • Authentication via managed auth provider; passwords stored only as salted hashes.
  • Regular platform updates and dependency maintenance; monitored infrastructure.
  • Backups and point-in-time recovery on the managed database [[confirm enabled]].
